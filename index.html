Background & philosophy

I'm an amateur landscape photographer from Raleigh, North Carolina that has always been captivated by the natural world. My photography journey began when I bought my Nikon D750 in April 2020 and slowly progressed from occasional shoots to a dedicated hobby. My journey will be documented in a blog coming soon, be sure to check that out on my webpage.

As someone who shoots with the intention of capturing the Earth's natural beauty, I strive for my photography to be representative of reality. I do make small adjustments to the light and color of my images to give them an immersive and gentle feel but try to avoid heavy distortions. I believe that the composition of objects in an image is the key to capturing and directing a viewer's attention. This style best represents my values as a photographer, but I am very appreciative of different philosophies and perspectives, my own will continue to evolve.

What I’m chasing
Nature's beauty represented in an immersive but realistic manner.

Nature is the story. I am the teller.
